# FamilyOS Project Charter

## Project codename

Project Hearthfire

## Product working name

FamilyOS

## Positioning

FamilyOS is the operating system for life at home.

It is designed for households, families, couples, co-parents, carers, flatmates and anyone who shares responsibilities with others.

## Mission

Reduce the invisible mental load of everyday life by helping households organise time, money, responsibilities, documents, care, goals and memories in one calm, intelligent place.

## North star

Reduce mental load.

Every product decision should make everyday life feel easier, calmer or more prepared.

## Non-negotiables

- Every feature must reduce mental load.
- Every common action should be easy to complete.
- Accessibility is part of the product from the beginning.
- AI must explain important decisions.
- Privacy comes before convenience.
- The product should support different household types.
- The app should prepare people for life, not keep them inside the app.
- Calm is a feature.

## Working model

FamilyOS is developed through two parallel tracks.

### Track A: Product Bible

The permanent source of truth for vision, principles, architecture, standards and product decisions.

### Track B: Product Factory

The practical delivery track for build packs, prototypes, design, engineering, AI prompts, QA and release planning.

## Immediate priorities

1. Establish the repository structure.
2. Complete the Product Bible foundation.
3. Create Build Pack 001 for Today.
4. Define the module documentation standard.
5. Create reusable prompts for design, engineering and AI agents.

## Quality bar

FamilyOS should feel premium, human, calm, intuitive and trustworthy. Every interaction should earn its place.

## Source of truth

GitHub is the permanent source of truth for this project. Chat is used for collaboration, ideation and drafting, but important decisions should be committed to the repository.
