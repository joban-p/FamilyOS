# FamilyOS Manifesto

Modern life at home has become too fragmented.

People are expected to remember everything: school letters, bills, appointments, shopping, documents, birthdays, pets, care responsibilities, household maintenance, plans, goals and the hundreds of tiny commitments that keep life moving.

Most software gives people more places to organise things.

FamilyOS exists to reduce the need to organise everything manually.

We believe people should not need five apps and a tired brain to get through a normal week.

We believe the best technology is quiet, useful and trustworthy.

We believe households come in many forms and every one of them deserves tools that respect real life.

We believe AI should remove work, not create more work.

We believe calm is a feature.

We believe accessibility is not optional.

We believe privacy is the foundation of trust.

We believe the product should prepare people for life, not keep them inside the app.

FamilyOS is being built to give people their mental space back.

That is the work.
