# Entity: Person

## Definition

A Person is an individual connected to one or more households.

A person may be an adult, child, carer, co-parent, guest, professional contact or household collaborator.

## Core attributes

- Person ID.
- Name.
- Role.
- Contact details.
- Date of birth where relevant.
- Permissions.
- Preferences.
- Accessibility needs where provided.
- Household memberships.

## Relationships

- Belongs to Household.
- Owns Tasks.
- Attends Events.
- Has Documents.
- Has Goals.
- May care for Person.
- May be responsible for Pet.

## AI behaviour

AI may personalise suggestions based on a person's role, schedule, preferences and responsibilities. Sensitive personal data should require explicit consent.
