# Entity: Household

## Definition

A Household is a group of one or more people who share responsibilities, living arrangements, finances, care duties, assets, documents or plans.

A household does not need to be a traditional family.

## Examples

- Family home.
- Couple.
- Co-parenting arrangement.
- Shared flat.
- Multi-generational home.
- Care group.

## Core attributes

- Household ID.
- Name.
- Type.
- Members.
- Roles.
- Time zone.
- Primary location.
- Created date.
- Privacy settings.

## Relationships

- Has many People.
- Has many Pets.
- Has many Events.
- Has many Tasks.
- Has many Documents.
- Has many Budgets.
- Has many Assets.
- Has many Goals.

## AI behaviour

AI can use household context to prioritise suggestions, reminders and automations. Sensitive insights should require appropriate permissions and clear explanation.
