# Entity: Event

## Definition

An Event is anything that happens at a point in time or across a period of time.

Events may be appointments, trips, school activities, bills, household commitments, celebrations or shared responsibilities.

## Core attributes

- Event ID.
- Title.
- Type.
- Start time.
- End time.
- Location.
- Participants.
- Related tasks.
- Related documents.
- Priority.
- Recurrence.

## Relationships

- Belongs to Household.
- Involves People.
- Occurs at Place.
- Has Tasks.
- Has Documents.
- May create Memory.
- May affect Budget.

## AI behaviour

AI can help detect preparation tasks, travel needs, conflicts, reminders and related documents.
