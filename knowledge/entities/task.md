# Entity: Task

## Definition

A Task is an action that needs to be completed by a person, household or system.

Tasks may be manual, assigned, automated, recurring or generated from captured information.

## Core attributes

- Task ID.
- Title.
- Description.
- Owner.
- Household.
- Due date.
- Priority.
- Status.
- Related entities.
- Created source.

## Relationships

- Belongs to Household.
- Assigned to Person.
- Relates to Event.
- Relates to Document.
- Relates to Asset.
- Relates to Goal.

## AI behaviour

AI can suggest task titles, due dates, owners and related entities. AI-created tasks should remain editable and reversible.
