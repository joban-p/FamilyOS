# Entity: Document

## Definition

A Document is a stored file, scan, image, PDF, letter, contract, receipt, certificate or other captured record that may relate to household life.

## Core attributes

- Document ID.
- Title.
- Type.
- Original file.
- Extracted text.
- Source.
- Date captured.
- Linked entities.
- Sensitivity level.
- Expiry date where relevant.
- Owner.

## Relationships

- Belongs to Household.
- Relates to Person.
- Relates to Asset.
- Relates to Event.
- Relates to Expense.
- Relates to Task.
- Generates Reminder.

## AI behaviour

AI can classify documents, extract useful dates, identify related entities and suggest automations. High-sensitivity documents should use stricter privacy rules.
