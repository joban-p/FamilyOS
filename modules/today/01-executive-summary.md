# 01 Executive Summary

## Module

Today

## Build Pack

Build Pack 001

## Summary

Today is the daily starting point for FamilyOS.

It replaces the traditional app dashboard with a calm, intelligent briefing that helps the household understand what matters now, what needs attention next and what can safely wait.

Today brings together calendar events, tasks, reminders, bills, family responsibilities, travel, weather, care schedules, pet needs, household commitments, goals, memories and AI-generated recommendations into one prioritised surface.

The experience should feel like a thoughtful person in the household has already checked the day and prepared the most useful summary.

## Primary user problem

People do not struggle because they lack information. They struggle because information is scattered, easy to forget and difficult to prioritise.

Today solves this by turning fragmented household information into a clear daily briefing.

## Core user need

I need to know what matters today without checking five different apps.

## Product goal

Make FamilyOS worth opening every morning.

## Experience goal

The user should gain value in under 10 seconds and complete the most likely action in under 30 seconds.

## Strategic importance

Today is the habit-forming surface of FamilyOS. It is where users build trust, where AI proves value and where the product becomes part of everyday life.

## In scope for MVP

- Daily briefing.
- Prioritised focus card.
- Timeline of today's commitments.
- AI priorities.
- Quick capture.
- Family pulse cards.
- Continue section for active projects.
- Recent household activity.
- Empty calm state.

## Out of scope for MVP

- Full conversational AI chat.
- Smart home automation.
- Advanced wearable experiences.
- Vehicle integration.
- Predictive healthcare insights.
- Complex household scoring.

## Success statement

Today succeeds when users open it in the morning and think: I know what matters now.
