# 02 Product Vision

## Vision

Create the most useful first screen in any household management product.

Today should prepare people for real life. It should help users understand the day ahead, avoid forgotten responsibilities, make better decisions and feel more in control.

## Product philosophy

Today is not a dashboard.

A dashboard displays information.

Today prepares the household.

The difference is important. Today should not simply show everything the system knows. It should decide what is most useful right now and present it with calm confidence.

## What Today should answer

- What matters today?
- What needs action?
- What can wait?
- What might I forget?
- Is anything urgent?
- Is my household on track?
- What should I do next?

## What Today should not become

- A cluttered dashboard.
- A feed of every household update.
- A notification centre.
- A list of widgets.
- A place that requires configuration.
- A screen that creates more admin.

## Experience principles

### Prepare, do not distract

The user should leave Today and get on with life.

### Prioritise, do not display everything

Only the most useful information should appear.

### Explain, do not overwhelm

AI should explain why something matters when appropriate.

### Adapt, do not surprise

Today should adjust to time, context and household state while remaining predictable.

### Reassure, do not pressure

The tone should be calm, helpful and supportive.

## Long-term ambition

Today should become as habitual as checking the weather, but more valuable because it brings together the commitments, context and responsibilities that actually shape household life.

## North star outcome

The user should feel: I am ready for today.
