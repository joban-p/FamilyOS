# 03 Problem Statement

## Problem

Household life is fragmented across too many apps, channels and mental notes.

A person may need to check a calendar for events, a banking app for bills, a notes app for lists, WhatsApp for family coordination, email for school updates, reminders for tasks and a weather app before leaving home.

The problem is not a lack of information.

The problem is that the information is scattered, disconnected and poorly prioritised.

## Human problem

People wake up carrying invisible questions.

- What have I forgotten?
- Is anything due today?
- Who needs to be where?
- Can we afford this week?
- What needs doing before school or work?
- What did my partner add yesterday?
- Is there anything important in email?
- What should I focus on first?

These questions create cognitive load before the day has properly started.

## Existing behaviour

Most households work around this problem using a patchwork of tools.

- Shared calendars.
- Notes.
- WhatsApp groups.
- Reminder apps.
- Banking apps.
- School apps.
- Paper notes.
- Memory.

The burden remains with the person. They still need to remember where information lives, check multiple places and decide what matters.

## Why existing apps fail

Most apps are organised around their own feature category.

Calendar apps show events.
Budget apps show money.
Task apps show tasks.
Weather apps show weather.
Messaging apps show conversations.

Real life does not work in categories.

A single morning may involve school, traffic, weather, bills, food, pets, work and family responsibilities.

Today exists because users need one calm place that turns fragmented information into a useful daily briefing.

## Opportunity

FamilyOS can become the trusted morning layer above all household information.

If Today consistently tells users what matters, users will return because it creates immediate value.

## Product hypothesis

If FamilyOS can provide a calm, prioritised briefing in under 10 seconds, households will trust it as their daily starting point and rely on it to reduce forgotten responsibilities, repeated checking and decision fatigue.

## Desired behavioural change

From:

I need to check everything.

To:

FamilyOS will tell me what matters.
