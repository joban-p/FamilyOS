# 05 Jobs To Be Done

## Purpose

This document defines the functional, emotional and social jobs that Today must support.

Today should not be designed around features. It should be designed around the real jobs people need help with at the start and throughout the day.

## Primary Job To Be Done

When I start my day, I want FamilyOS to tell me what matters across my household so that I feel prepared without checking multiple apps.

## Functional jobs

### Understand the day quickly

When I open the app, I want to see today's most important commitments so that I know what needs attention first.

### Avoid forgetting important tasks

When something matters today or soon, I want it surfaced clearly so that I do not have to carry it in my head.

### Coordinate household responsibilities

When responsibilities involve more than one person, I want to see who is doing what so that nothing falls through the gaps.

### Prepare for events

When an event needs preparation, I want related tasks, documents, locations and reminders connected to it so that I am not caught out later.

### Capture new information instantly

When I remember something, receive a document or notice a task, I want to capture it immediately so that it is not lost.

### Decide what can wait

When there are many possible tasks, I want FamilyOS to highlight what matters most so that I can focus without feeling overwhelmed.

## Emotional jobs

### Feel calm

I want to start the day without anxiety about what I might have forgotten.

### Feel in control

I want to trust that the household is organised and that important things will be surfaced.

### Feel supported

I want FamilyOS to feel like another responsible adult has checked the day with me.

### Feel reassured

I want to know that if nothing urgent appears, things are genuinely under control.

## Social jobs

### Reduce household friction

I want shared responsibilities to be visible so that my partner, co-parent, housemate or carer does not have to chase me.

### Build trust between household members

I want everyone to know what is happening so that coordination feels fair and transparent.

### Support different household types

I want the app to work whether I am in a couple, co-parenting, caring for relatives, living with flatmates or managing a family.

## Outcome statements

Today should help users say:

- I know what matters today.
- I know what I need to do next.
- I know who is responsible.
- I know what can wait.
- I trust FamilyOS to remember for me.

## Product implication

The Today experience should be built around prioritisation, preparation and confidence, not information volume.
