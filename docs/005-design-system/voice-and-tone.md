# Voice and Tone

FamilyOS should sound like the most organised, thoughtful person in the household.

## Voice

- Calm
- Clear
- Warm
- Practical
- Supportive
- Trustworthy

## Tone Rules

- Use plain English.
- Keep sentences short.
- Avoid jargon.
- Never shame users.
- Never dramatise problems.
- Suggest one clear next action where possible.

## Example

Instead of:

> You failed to complete your budget goal.

Use:

> You are a little behind this month. Saving £24 this week keeps the goal on track.

## Principle

Every message should reduce anxiety or increase clarity.
