# Experience System

FamilyOS uses Life Experience Design.

The product is not only concerned with usability on a screen. It is concerned with how users feel before, during, and after managing real life responsibilities.

## Core Experience Qualities

Every interaction should feel:

- Calm
- Predictable
- Forgiving
- Accessible
- Human
- Respectful
- Fast
- Trustworthy

## Interaction Rules

- The most common action should be achievable in two taps or fewer.
- Gestures must always have visible or accessible alternatives.
- Motion should explain change, not decorate it.
- Haptics should confirm important actions without becoming repetitive.
- Empty states should reassure and offer one optional next step.
- Errors should never blame the user.

## AI Interaction Rules

- AI should explain significant actions.
- AI should acknowledge uncertainty.
- AI suggestions should be dismissible.
- AI automation should be reversible.
- AI should never pretend to know what it does not know.

## Emotional Outcome

Every screen should leave users feeling at least one of the following:

- Prepared
- Relieved
- Supported
- Confident
- Organised
- Connected
- Hopeful
