# Experience Principles

FamilyOS is not designed to help people organise their lives.

It is designed so they do not have to carry everything in their head.

## ACTIVE Framework

Every experience should follow the ACTIVE framework.

### Anticipate

Predict needs before users have to ask.

### Capture

Allow anything important to enter the system in under five seconds.

### Think

Use AI and context to organise, classify, suggest, and prepare.

### Integrate

Connect information across calendars, money, documents, people, places, assets, tasks, and memories.

### Visualise

Make information understandable in seconds.

### Empower

Support users without judgement, shame, or pressure.

## Experience Quality Tests

Every screen should pass:

- The five second test
- The two tap test
- The one minute test
- The grandma test
- The Monday morning test
- The mental load test

If a screen fails, it should be redesigned.
