# FamilyOS Product Bible

The Product Bible is the strategic, product and experience foundation for FamilyOS.

It explains why the product exists, what problems it solves, how it should behave, and how each module should be designed and built.

9. Living Design System
10. Product Evolution Map & Module Catalogue
11. AI Operating Principles

## Working principle

Every feature should reduce mental load, increase confidence and make everyday family life easier to manage.
