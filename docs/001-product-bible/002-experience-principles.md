# Document 002: Experience Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the experience principles that guide FamilyOS.

FamilyOS is not designed to help people organise their lives. It is designed so they do not have to carry so much of the mental burden themselves.

Every interaction, automation and notification should remove work rather than create it.

## Philosophy

FamilyOS should behave like the most organised, thoughtful person in the household.

It should quietly notice, remember, prepare and help without asking unnecessary questions or demanding attention.

## The ACTIVE framework

Every experience should follow the ACTIVE framework.

### Anticipate

Predict needs before the user has to remember them.

Examples include passport expiry, upcoming school holidays, bills, car maintenance and family commitments.

### Capture

Everything should enter FamilyOS in under five seconds.

Supported inputs include voice, photo, screenshot, PDF, email, QR code, calendar invite, receipt, document and shared link.

### Think

AI should interpret context, classify information and suggest the next best action without forcing users through forms.

### Integrate

Nothing exists in isolation. A receipt can become an expense, warranty, asset, budget entry and reminder.

### Visualise

Information should be understandable in seconds through hierarchy, timelines, simple progress and clear visual cues.

### Empower

The app should never shame users. It should support progress, confidence and small achievable actions.

## FamilyOS laws

1. Never ask for information the system can discover.
2. Never make users organise data manually when AI can do it safely.
3. Every interaction must reduce mental load.
4. Every screen should answer one clear question.
5. Never interrupt without value.
6. Progress beats perfection.
7. Privacy comes before convenience.
8. Every household is different.

## Quality tests

Every experience should pass:

- The five second test.
- The two tap test.
- The one minute test.
- The accessibility test.
- The mental load test.
- The Monday morning test.

## Emotional outcome

Every screen should leave users feeling more prepared, calm, supported, confident or connected.
