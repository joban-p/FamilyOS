# Document 001: Product Vision & Strategy

## Status

Draft v0.1

## Working product name

FamilyOS

## Vision statement

To become the operating system that every household relies on to organise, simplify and improve everyday life.

## Mission statement

FamilyOS exists to reduce the invisible mental load carried by individuals, couples and families.

We achieve this by bringing planning, finance, communication, caregiving and household management into one calm, intelligent experience.

Our products help people spend less time organising life and more time living it.

## Executive summary

Modern life has become increasingly fragmented. Families rely on disconnected applications to manage finances, calendars, shopping, childcare, household administration, communication and long-term planning. Each application solves an individual problem, but none address the underlying challenge: the growing mental load required to coordinate everyday life.

FamilyOS exists to reduce that mental load.

Rather than becoming another family organiser, FamilyOS aims to become the operating system for modern family life. It provides a single trusted place where households can organise time, finances, responsibilities, memories and goals while intelligently connecting information that would otherwise remain isolated.

Artificial intelligence is not treated as a standalone feature. It is an invisible layer that quietly organises information, predicts future needs, automates repetitive tasks and helps families make better decisions without increasing complexity.

## Product belief

People should not have to remember everything that keeps life running.

## North star

Reduce mental load.

Every feature, workflow and interaction should reduce mental effort, increase confidence or save meaningful time.

## Product principles

1. Remove mental load.
2. Capture information in under five seconds.
3. Automate whenever confidence is high.
4. Explain important AI decisions.
5. Design for calm, not engagement.
6. Never require duplicate data entry.
7. Make collaboration effortless.
8. Respect privacy by default.
9. Support every type of household.
10. Leave users feeling more in control than when they opened the app.

## Success metrics

- Time saved per household.
- Tasks automated by AI.
- Weekly active households.
- Household confidence score.
- Family satisfaction score.
- Monthly retention.
- Net Promoter Score.
- Reduction in repeated manual entry.

## Success statement

FamilyOS succeeds when users say: I do not need to remember that anymore.
