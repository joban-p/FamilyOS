# FamilyOS Manifesto

Family life should not require project management.

Parents should not need six apps to organise one school week.

Couples should not argue because nobody remembered the water bill.

Co-parents should not rely on scattered messages, screenshots, and memory to coordinate care.

Households should not need separate tools for calendars, bills, pets, documents, tasks, trips, assets, routines, and goals.

Technology should quietly carry the work people are already carrying in their heads.

FamilyOS exists to give people back time, attention, confidence, and peace of mind.

We do not optimise for screen time.

We optimise for life feeling easier.
