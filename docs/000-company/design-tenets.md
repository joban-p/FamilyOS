# Design Tenets

These tenets guide every screen, component, flow, and product decision.

## Tenets

1. One thought per screen.
2. Show what matters now.
3. Hide complexity until it is useful.
4. Make the next action obvious.
5. Use plain English.
6. Avoid shame, guilt, and pressure.
7. Use motion to explain, not decorate.
8. Use colour for meaning, not noise.
9. Make empty states feel calm, not broken.
10. Design for one-handed use.
11. Make the common action fast.
12. Make the risky action reversible.
13. Ask fewer questions over time.
14. Explain AI decisions clearly.
15. Respect quiet days.
16. Treat family data as sensitive by default.
17. Design for children, adults, carers, grandparents, and guests.
18. Make progress visible without turning life into a game.
19. Create confidence within ten seconds.
20. Leave users feeling lighter than when they arrived.
