# Mission

FamilyOS exists to reduce the invisible mental load carried by individuals, couples, families, carers, and shared households.

We do this by creating thoughtful software that brings planning, finance, co-parenting, household management, documents, goals, communication, and memory into one calm and intelligent experience.

The mission is not to make people more productive.

The mission is to help people feel more prepared, more connected, and more in control of everyday life.
