# Product Principles

## 1. Reduce Mental Load

Every feature must reduce remembering, coordination, decision fatigue, preparation effort, or anxiety.

## 2. Capture First, Organise Later

Users should be able to add anything in under five seconds. The system organises it afterwards.

## 3. The Household Is the User

FamilyOS is designed for relationships, not isolated individuals.

## 4. Calm Is a Feature

The product should feel quiet, prepared, and trustworthy. It should never feel demanding.

## 5. AI Works Quietly

AI should support, suggest, automate, and explain. It should not become another thing users need to manage.

## 6. One Source of Truth

Important household information should not be duplicated across disconnected places.

## 7. Trust Before Convenience

Privacy, consent, access control, reversibility, and explainability must come before automation.

## 8. Accessible by Default

FamilyOS should meet WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum and aim higher where practical.

## 9. Progress Beats Perfection

The product should encourage progress without shaming missed tasks, broken habits, or messy family life.

## 10. Every Interaction Earns Its Place

Nothing should exist because competitors have it. Everything exists because it makes life easier.
