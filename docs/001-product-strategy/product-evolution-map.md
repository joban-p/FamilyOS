# Product Evolution Map

FamilyOS should not be treated as a fixed roadmap of features.

It should be treated as a product that evolves with the household.

## Stage 1: Daily Life

Focus on immediate daily value.

- Today
- Inbox
- Calendar
- Lists
- Shared tasks
- Bills
- Basic finances
- Household members

## Stage 2: Household Management

Support shared responsibilities and practical family life.

- Co-parenting schedules
- Bill splitting
- Goals
- Income and expense tracking
- Assets and liabilities
- Household inventory
- Documents
- Pet profiles
- Trips

## Stage 3: Family Intelligence

Use the relationship engine and AI to reduce mental load.

- Smart suggestions
- Predictive reminders
- AI planning
- Family knowledge graph
- Natural language search
- Smart capture
- Household state engine

## Stage 4: Life Platform

Expand into broader life infrastructure.

- Smart home
- Insurance
- Investments
- Education
- Health integrations
- Vehicle management
- Marketplace
- Developer APIs

## Stage 5: Legacy

Preserve family knowledge, memories, documents, and history.

- Family timeline
- Digital legacy
- Estate planning
- Memory archive
- Life events
- Generational records

## Long-Term Principle

Users should not outgrow FamilyOS.

The product should become more useful as life becomes more complex.
