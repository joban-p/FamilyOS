# FamilyOS Constitution

## Purpose

The FamilyOS Constitution defines the principles that should not change as the product evolves.

It is the highest product authority in the repository. If a future decision conflicts with this document, the decision should be challenged.

## Article I: Mental space

FamilyOS exists to give people their mental space back.

The product should reduce cognitive load, reduce repeated remembering and make life at home feel easier to manage.

## Article II: Household first

The household is the primary customer, not only the individual.

FamilyOS should support families, couples, co-parents, carers, flatmates, shared homes and multi-generational households.

## Article III: One input, many outcomes

A single capture should create as many useful outcomes as possible.

Users should not need to enter the same information repeatedly.

## Article IV: Capture before organisation

Users should be able to capture information before deciding what it is, where it belongs or who owns it.

Organisation is the responsibility of FamilyOS whenever confidence is high enough.

## Article V: AI removes work

AI exists to quietly remove work, not create more work.

AI should explain important decisions, ask only when needed and never pretend to know something it does not know.

## Article VI: Calm is a feature

Calm is not an aesthetic preference. It is a product requirement.

FamilyOS should reduce anxiety, avoid unnecessary notifications and never optimise for screen time over quality of life.

## Article VII: Accessibility is mandatory

Accessibility is a design and engineering requirement from the beginning.

FamilyOS should meet WCAG 2.2 AA as a minimum and aim higher where practical.

## Article VIII: Privacy creates trust

Family data is sensitive.

Privacy, consent, transparency, encryption and user control are fundamental to the product.

## Article IX: Every interaction earns its place

Every button, card, notification and automation must reduce friction, increase confidence or support a meaningful household outcome.

If it does not, it should not exist.

## Article X: People before technology

Technology serves people. Never the other way around.

FamilyOS should help people live better lives at home, not turn family life into administration.
