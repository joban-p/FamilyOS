# ADR-001: Why Today Is Not a Dashboard

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Most productivity and family apps use dashboards to show a collection of widgets, lists and charts. Dashboards often show information because it is available, not because it is useful in the current moment.

FamilyOS is designed to reduce mental load. A generic dashboard risks increasing cognitive effort by making users scan, interpret and prioritise too much information.

## Decision

The primary daily surface is called Today and should behave as a daily briefing rather than a dashboard.

Today should curate, rank and explain what matters now.

## Consequences

- Today must be selective.
- Information is ranked by relevance and urgency.
- Empty or calm days should not be filled with unnecessary content.
- AI is responsible for helping prioritise what appears.
- Success is measured by confidence gained, not time spent.
