# Living Scenario: The School Letter

Emma is making dinner.

Her son walks into the kitchen holding a folded piece of paper.

Do not forget this.

Normally, the letter would be placed on the kitchen counter, buried under other papers and remembered too late.

Instead, Emma opens FamilyOS, photographs the letter and closes the app.

The whole interaction takes four seconds.

While she continues cooking, FamilyOS reads the document, detects that it relates to school, identifies the date, extracts the payment deadline, links the item to her child, stores the original document and suggests a reminder.

Emma does not complete a form.

She does not choose a category.

She does not file the document.

She simply captures it.

FamilyOS takes responsibility for the next steps.

## Mental load removed

- Remembering the letter.
- Deciding where to save it.
- Reading it immediately while busy.
- Manually creating calendar events.
- Manually creating reminders.
- Linking it to the right child.

## Product benchmark

If Inbox cannot make this moment effortless, it is not good enough.
